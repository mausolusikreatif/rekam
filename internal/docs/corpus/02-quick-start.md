Three steps: make an account, connect an agent to it, write something worth
remembering.

## 1. Create an account

Sign up with an email address and a password. rekam emails you a confirmation
code; paste it in and you land in your catalog — empty, which is the right
starting point.

The account is free while rekam is in beta. Nothing is metered yet.

## 2. Connect an agent

Add rekam as a connector in the assistant you use. It opens a rekam login in a
new window, you sign in as yourself, and the connection is authorized against
your own memory — not a shared one.

If you belong to more than one workspace, you pick which one the connection may
read and write. A connection is pinned to exactly one workspace; to connect a
second, authorize again. See [[The MCP endpoint]] for what the agent can do
once it is connected, and [[Working with other people]] for what a workspace is.

## 3. Write your first record

Anything you would otherwise repeat to a model every session: a deploy runbook,
the reason a library was rejected, the shape of your database, how your team
names things.

Give it a title, a path in [[The taxonomy]], and a body. Connect it to whatever
it relates to with [[Wiki-links]]. Your agent can do all three itself once it is
connected — and every record it writes carries your name on it, which is
[[Authorship and history]].
