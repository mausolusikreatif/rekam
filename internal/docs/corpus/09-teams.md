A corpus can be yours alone or shared with a team. The setup is the same either
way, so starting solo costs nothing later.

## Roles

An Owner controls the team and its membership. An Admin manages members. An
Editor reads and writes within granted branches of [[The taxonomy]]. A Viewer
reads those branches and writes nothing.

Grants are per-branch, which means a team can hold drafts an agent cannot see
alongside records everyone reads.

## Switching between corpora

Your own records and each team you belong to are separate corpora. You choose
which one you are working in, and agents do the same — a write goes where you
pointed it, never to whichever store happened to be open.

## Starting solo

Nothing needs migrating when a second person arrives. Create a team, move or
copy what should be shared, and keep the rest personal. Attribution follows the
records across, so [[Authorship and history]] still answers.
